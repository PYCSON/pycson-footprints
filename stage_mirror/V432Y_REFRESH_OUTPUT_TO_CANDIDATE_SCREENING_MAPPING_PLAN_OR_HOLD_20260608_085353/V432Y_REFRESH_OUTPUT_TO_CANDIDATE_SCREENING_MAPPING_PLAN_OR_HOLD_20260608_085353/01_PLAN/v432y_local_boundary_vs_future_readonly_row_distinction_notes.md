# Local Boundary Versus Future Readonly Rows

Local/package-boundary rows are validation evidence only. They may test manifest shape, trace fields, and boundary checks, but they must not be promoted into candidate signals.

Future real readonly rows require explicit approval before any source access and must preserve no-login, no-cookie, no-captcha, no-proxy, no-bypass, no account automation, no DATA_BRIDGE write, no active payload write, no EV calculation, and no BUY/TRADE/ORDER boundaries.
